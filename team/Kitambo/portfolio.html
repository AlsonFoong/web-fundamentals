About Me

My journey into technology has been a journey of curiosity, growth, and continuous learning.

Growing up, I was always fascinated by people who understood computers and technology. I admired how they could create things, solve problems, and build solutions using something that seemed so complex. However, I did not have much exposure to computers while growing up, and I only started using computers more actively after completing high school.

My curiosity eventually led me to pursue a Bachelor of Computer Science at Sunway University in Malaysia. Being accepted into this program was an exciting milestone because Computer Science was the field I wanted to explore. I joined this journey not because I already knew everything about programming, but because I wanted to learn, challenge myself, and understand how technology works.

Throughout my studies, I have explored programming languages and technologies such as Python, Java, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Each assignment and project has helped me become more confident and independent as a developer. I have learned that programming is not only about writing code, but also about problem-solving, patience, creativity, and continuous improvement.

As I continue my Computer Science journey, my goal is to become a skilled software developer who can create meaningful and innovative solutions. I am excited to continue learning, building projects, and discovering how far I can grow in this field.
